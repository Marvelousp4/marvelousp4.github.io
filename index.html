Robotics Integrations Engineer · Tesla

Linhao Bai

I’m a Robotics Integrations Engineer at Tesla, working on full-stack production robotics systems that connect fleet-level intelligence, robot software, and physical automation.

My work takes an end-to-end view of robotic systems: from fleet orchestration and distributed services to robot runtime, onboard software, autonomy interfaces, hardware-software interaction, and reliability under real production constraints.

My technical interests span robotics software, distributed systems, localization and controls, observability and evaluation, and robot learning. At Georgia Tech, my research focused on dexterous manipulation, sim-to-real, visuomotor learning, and real-hardware policy execution.

Earlier in my career, I worked on robot-controller software and international AMR systems at SEER Robotics and Wellwit Robotics, giving me experience across both robot-level software and large-scale real-world robotics systems.
